I've been building things on the side for years: a blackjack game that started as a university Unity project, a SwiftUI rewrite of it, and a multiplayer party game for iOS. Most of what I learned along the way lived in my head or in commit messages.

This site is where that changes. Each project gets a write-up covering what I built, the decisions behind it, what went wrong, and what I'd do differently.

## What's coming

- **Perfect Pair Blackjack** — from Unity and C# to the browser, then to native SwiftUI
- **Drag-and-drop in SwiftUI** — building chip betting with `DragGesture` and `PreferenceKey`
- **Multiplayer lobbies** — join codes, deep links and keeping the backend swappable

Thanks for reading.
